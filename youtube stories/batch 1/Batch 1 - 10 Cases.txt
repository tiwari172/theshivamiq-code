THESHIVAMIQ – LONG VIDEO 01 – 10 CASES
=====================================
Mix: 5 MEDIUM + 3 HARD + 2 VERY HARD  (video mein isi order mein, aasaan se mushkil)
Har case ko DETECTIVE SHIVAM solve karta hai (police nahi). Saboot Shivam khud dhoondhta, padhta aur dikhata hai.
Har case: HOOK -> SABOOT (pehle, casually, 1-1 sec) -> BAYAAN (8-10 suspects) -> SHIVAM ka galat ishara -> SAWAAL
Niyam: sirf EK bayaan kisi saboot / pakke fact se takraata hai = killer. Baaki sab bayaan saboot se mel khaate hain.
Har masoom ke paas ek "shak wali baat" (red herring) hai. Killer ka number har case mein alag.
Jawab video mein KABHI nahi. Jawab agle hafte ki video ki shuruaat mein.
VO (Devanagari) video banate waqt isi se likhenge. Captions Roman Hinglish.

Killer ka number:  C1=7  C2=2  C3=9  C4=6  C5=4  C6=1  C7=10  C8=3  C9=8  C10=5
