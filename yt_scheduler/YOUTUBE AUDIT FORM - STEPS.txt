STEP 3 - SUBMIT KE BAAD
-----------------------------------------------------------
- Email aayega (kabhi spam folder mein bhi). YouTube API team sawaal
  pooch sakti hai - jaldi aur seedha jawab do, isi txt ki baatein use karo.
- Woh "YouTube API Services Terms / Developer Policies" follow karne ko
  bol sakte hain - is tool mein koi problem wali cheez nahi hai.
- Approve hone ka email aaye -> "2 TEST 1 VIDEO.bat" se ek video daalo ->
  Studio mein check karo ki lock nahi hai aur "Scheduled" dikh raha hai.
- Tab tak: videos haath se upload karo (title/description/tags Excel se copy).
