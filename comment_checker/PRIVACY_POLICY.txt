Privacy Policy – theshivamiq comment checker
Last updated: 18 October 2026

1. Who we are
"theshivamiq comment checker" is a private tool run by the owner of the Instagram account @theshivamiq. It is used only by that account owner. It is not a public app and nobody else can log in to it.

2. What data the tool reads
Using the official Instagram API, the tool reads only data from the @theshivamiq account itself:
- the account's own posts and reels (caption, link, date, like/comment counts and insights)
- public comments and replies left on those posts (commenter's Instagram username, comment text and time)
The tool does not read private messages, passwords, email addresses, phone numbers, follower lists or any data from other accounts.

3. Why the data is used
Comments are checked against the correct answer of each detective quiz reel so that the account owner can count correct answers, make a leaderboard of top players and give them shoutouts. Post insights are used only to see how each reel performed.

4. Storage and sharing
The data is saved only on the account owner's personal computer in a spreadsheet. It is not sold, rented or shared with anyone, and it is not used for advertising. A public shoutout may mention the Instagram username of a top player, which is already public on Instagram.

5. How long data is kept
Data is kept as long as the leaderboard is running. It can be deleted at any time.

6. Deleting your data
If you commented on @theshivamiq and want your username and comments removed from the leaderboard, send a Direct Message to @theshivamiq on Instagram or an email to the address below with the subject "Delete my data". Your data will be removed within 7 days. Deleting your comment on Instagram also stops it from being counted in future.
